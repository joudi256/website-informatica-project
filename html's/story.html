Our Story

La Dolce Vita began as a dream between two lifelong friends, Marco and Luca, who shared a passion for authentic Italian cuisine. Inspired by the rich flavors of their hometowns, they wanted to bring a taste of Italy to Vught.

From humble beginnings in a small kitchen, they focused on using fresh ingredients, traditional recipes, and a welcoming atmosphere. Every dish reflects their dedication to quality and love for food.

Over the years, La Dolce Vita has grown into a beloved spot for families, couples, and friends, where each meal is crafted with care and every guest is treated like part of the family.

"Our goal has always been simple: create memorable meals and a place where people feel at home," says Marco.

Whether you're here for a special occasion or a casual dinner, we hope you experience the warmth, flavor, and joy that inspired us to open La Dolce Vita. Across all of our locations in europe, we strive to maintain the same level of quality and hospitality that started it all.
